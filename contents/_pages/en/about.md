About Us
========

> We make soundtracks, sound design and sound art installations.
> Our objective is to make elegant, pure and beautiful works by creating, developing and supporting projects having an aim in propagating knowledge & culture or expressing human feelings & emotions.
> We achieve it by employing computer technologies coupled with our subjective aesthetic.
>
> Contact us [info@bubblefish.studio](mailto:info@bubblefish.studio) for enquiries and collaborations.

We are a team of two sound artists with a background in Computer Science.
We believe Art and Engineering are two sides of the same coin: computer science, computer graphics, digital signal processing and audio technology are all important tools that can help us create beautiful music and sound installations fitting different and unique environments.
Nonetheless, it's only thanks to our intuition, experience and sense of beauty that we can incept meaningful works.
Our focus is on the following types of project:

- soundtracks for backgrounds, supporting exhibitions or enhancing locations
- computational, generative, interactive music installations
- creative programming for multimedia interactive designs
- computer music research projects

## Yilin

Why do I want to make art? This is the question I ask myself, and my answer is because I want to resonate with people, and want my work to be seen. 
I will explore the world, with the desire to create. So I infuse my work with what I see, hear and feel.

In 2020, we went to a project site located in an ancient church, there was not much people in the backyard as the rest of the project team was busy with other things. And I remember the moment when I pressed the play button off the speaker to let the music we wrote flow out of the little box, slowly filling the whole space. Later people came and left with a light smile. I remember the magic of that beautiful moment, and I want to create more.

## Matteo

I grew up in a small and isolated town by the seaside. I've been making music since I was ten years old, with an approach having two faces:
the first is to share my own feelings with other people and hope to communicate using a common language;
the second is to just play around and explore its possibilities.

I like challenging myself to learn new things in a self-taught approach. Nonetheless, I decided to follow a path in Engineering in order to strengthen my methodologies in realizing projects and ideas. In particular, I studied Computer Science and Musing Engineering in university, so I could combine my passions in technology and music making together in order to explore new forms of art.
