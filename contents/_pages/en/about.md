About Us
========

> Bubble Fish Studio is a multidisciplinary music studio.

We are focused on making applied music and sound design for short films, museum exhibitions, art installations, indoor and outdoor spaces.
Our objective is to make elegant, pure and beautiful works by creating, developing and supporting projects having an aim in propagating knowledge & culture or expressing human feelings & emotions.
Our music production projects incorporate a computer-assisted approach in addition to a subjective aesthetic.

Contact us [info@bubblefish.studio](mailto:info@bubblefish.studio) for more info!

## Fields of Interest

- applied music for different purposes and different places or circumstances
- computational, generative, interactive music and graphical applications/installations
- creative programming and multimedia interactive designs
- computer aided musical research projects

## Art and Engineering

Art and engineering are one and the same. We want to design beautiful music and sounds to suit different environments and needs, and this work requires outstanding technical and engineering skills to make it happen. Computer science, graphics, digital signal processing and audio technology will help us bring our imagination to life and make the end result interesting and rewarding.

It is fitting that the members of this studio, [Yilin](https://instagram.com/yilinzhu0) and [Matteo](https://teobe.net), are both pursuing MSc degrees of Music Engineering in Politecnico di Milano.
