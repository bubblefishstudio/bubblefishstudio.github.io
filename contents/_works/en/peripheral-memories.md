We like to do all sorts of interesting projects, but this is the first sound art project to be exhibited in a museum, Trieste, Nov.13 - 28, 2021. History passed down from generation to generation and does not dissipate, and even as the smoke of war drifts across this border land, the memories of the people remain.

Yilin have produced 4 musical pieces for this exhibition, each focusing on the historical and cultural heritage of the region: the naval industry, coffee industry, textile industry and mining industry. The style of the compositions is ambient and experimental. Several real samples were recorded (ship engine, coffee beans, birds, sewing machine, etc.) and combined with other music elements.

## Background

The exhibition features artworks from international artists who, after a stay in the area of Udine, Monfalcone and Trieste, cooperated with the local realities and gathered material and inspiration for their works. 

The exhibition revolves around the concept of cultural heritage of the region Friuli-Venezia Giulia in Italy, especially after World War II. People living in the balkanic borders of the Italian territory always had a complex relation between their own values and rulers' policies that succeeded throughout history. The B#Side project focuses on the de-industralization effects caused by the end of WW2, after the area has been exploited for military purposes (siderurgic production for weapons and ships, textile for military uniforms and coats, etc).

The placement of the installation has the purpose to attract visitors from outside the building and create a non-obstrusive atmosphere for visitors inside the building.

![Mixing on computer](/assets/works/peripheral-memories/mixing.png)

A specific on-site mixing and mastering has been done to make the soundtracks fit with the acoustics of the environment. In particular, low frequencies were cut to avoid rumbling and resonances, every track has been compressed to achieve comparable LUFS levels and dynamic range, in order to create a uniform experience across the tracks.
