This project is a composition of background music for the cultural heritage exhibition B#Side Peripheral Memories by IoDeposito Ngo.

The exhibition revolves around the cultural heritage of the region Friuli-Venezia Giulia in Italy, especially after World War II. The balkanic area of the Italian peninsula always had a complex relation between their own culture and invaders' policies. B#Side focuses on the de-industralization effects caused by the end of WW2, as the area was being exploited before that time for military purposes (siderurgic production for weapons, textile for military coats, etc).

![Entrance view of the exhibition](/assets/works/peripheral-memories/inside.png)

The exhibition focuses on four sectors: Naval, Coffee, Textile and Mining. These are the main areas in which the region is historically good at, and which were exploited during the war period. The exhibition features artworks from international artists who had a stay in the area of Udine, Monfalcone and Trieste. 

![Caption card for Yilin's installation](/assets/works/peripheral-memories/card.png)

Yilin's installation constitutes the common background music for the whole exhibition, featuring four distinct soundtracks, one for each sector.
The style of the compositions

The placement of the installation has the purpose to attract visitors from outside the building and create a non-obstrusive atmosphere for visitors inside the building. A specific on-site mixing has been done to make the soundtracks fit with the acoustics of the environment.
