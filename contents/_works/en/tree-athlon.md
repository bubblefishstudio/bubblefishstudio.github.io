Once November has passed, Christmas gets closer and closer. The lights go up in the streets, twinkling in all sorts of colors as night falls, reflecting on the masked faces of passers-by. Even when the north wind blows with the coldness of snow and ice, we still feel a fairy-tale sense of peace and joy.

*Everyone loves Christmas.*

For this Christmas tree competition we participated in, we used a tree provided by Politecnico di Milano, transforming it into a unique installation designed by us: original sound design and decorations. The installation was inspired by the cold winds of the Christmas season and we wanted to give to the viewer an aesthetically pleasing experience of a cold winter's day. At the same time, we wanted to reflect the harmony between the natural landscape and human festivity: the ribbon decorations hanging from the branches and trunks of the trees were made by our hands using recycled materials; we also minimized damage to the trees during the installation process.

The main decorations we put on the tree are the lights, the stripes and a water-proof speaker with an SD card to play the soundtrack we made.

## Background

This project is part of the [Polimi Tree-athlon Contest 2021](https://www.polimi.it/en/landingpages/tree-athlon/). The aim of the initiative is to decorate some of the trees located in the main spaces of the Leonardo, Bovisa and Territorial Campuses of Politecnico di Milano. Among the participants in the competition, the most original and innovative projects are selected, which will remain on display throughout the Christmas period.

![Polimi Tree-athlon Banner](/assets/works/tree-athlon/card.jpg)

We were selected to decorate two trees with the same design, one in the Bonardi Campus and another one in the Giuriati Sport Center.

## Sound Design

We composed an original soundtrack to be played through the speaker on the tree installation, creating an ambient windy but Christmas feeling music. When there’s some wind blowing, the audience can both see the dynamic changes of the stripes, and the lights at night, together with an original light music playing in the background.

The music is composed of three sections: The first sections is an opening created by the piano playing arpeggios over the circle of fifths, interlaced with wind sounds, creating a delightful and harmonical yet open and cold feeling. The second section contains cheerful music elements played by a music box, creating a more childish and happy feeling. The third section, finally, is a blurred version of the first section, where the wind blows away the musical elements, as winter passes by.

The installation, as a whole, acts as a bridge between human solitude and fragility against cold winter and nature, and human warmth connecting people hearts through festivity.
