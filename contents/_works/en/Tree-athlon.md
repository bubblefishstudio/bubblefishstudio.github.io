Once November has passed, Christmas gets closer and closer. The lights go up in the streets, twinkling in all sorts of colours as night falls, reflecting on the masked faces of passers-by. Even when the north wind blows with the coldness of snow and ice, you still feel a fairy-tale sense of peace and joy.

Everyone loves Christmas.

For this Christmas tree competition we participated for, we used a tree provided by the Politecnico di Milano, transforming it into a unique installation designed by us: original sound design and decorations.


The installation was inspired by the cold winds of the Christmas season and we wanted to give the viewer an aesthetically pleasing experience of a cold winter's day. At the same time, we wanted to reflect the harmony between the natural landscape and human festivity: the ribbon decorations hanging from the branches and trunks of the trees were made by our hand using recycled materials; we also minimised damage to the trees during the installation process. So, the main decorations we put on the tree are the lights, the stripes and a water-proof speaker with a SD card to play the soundtrack we made.

## Background

About Polimi Tree-athlon Contest 2021

> The aim of the initiative has been to decoratesome of the trees located in the main spaces of the Leonardo, Bovisa and Territorial Campuses of Politecnico di Milano. Among the participants in the competition, the most original and innovative projects have selected, which will remain on display throughout the Christmasperiod.

![Polimi Tree-athlon img][https://www.polimi.it/fileadmin/user_upload/Landing_page/Tree-Athlon/dicembre-2021/banner-contest-sito-eng.jpg]

## Sound Design

We composed an original soundtrack to be played through the speaker on the tree installation, creating an ambient windy but Christmas feeling music. When there’s some wind blowing, the audience can both see the dynamic changes of the stripes, and the lights at night, together with an original light music playing in the background.
