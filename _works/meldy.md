> Research Project in Music Emotion Recognition 2020, Online

Meldy is a web application that creates simple melody scores from a mood. Moods are described as valence and arousal on a 2D picker. Scores can be played in the browser as MIDI or downloaded as MusicXML.

Developed for Advanced Coding Tools and Methodologies and Computer Music: Representations and Models, Politecnico di Milano.
