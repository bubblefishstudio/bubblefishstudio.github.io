> Soundscape and Motion Graphics Installation 2022, Udine, Italy

*Inheritance* is a multimedia installation exhibited at Peripheral Memories II, Spazio 35, Udine, from 22 March to 9 April 2022.

After a short residency in Friuli, we made this sound artwork and a video from computer graphics and historical images. The piece holds traces of history, traditions, and legacies after the Second World War in the border area of Italy.
