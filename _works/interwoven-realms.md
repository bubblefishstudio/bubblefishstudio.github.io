Exhibition poster designed by Xiaoling Jin. Music and sound design by BubbleFish Studio.

APT Gallery, 6 Creekside, Deptford, London SE8 4SA. 3–13 August 2023, Thursday to Sunday, 12–5pm. 3D animation, 2:30.

*Interwoven Realms* invites you into a world where the boundaries between humans, nature, and dreams dissolve. Within this surreal realm, the human experience expands and intertwines with plants, animals, and objects to give birth to new creatures. This convergence mirrors the hidden depths that lie beneath the surface, where reality intertwines with imagination — the perceptions we project and the shadows we cast online, where identities are shaped and obscured.
