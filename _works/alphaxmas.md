> Generative Art Installation 2021, Online

AlphaXmas is a web installation: a growing Christmas tree and a generated carol in the background. Lights blink with the tempo. Each load of the page makes a unique tree and melody; occasionally a star appears when growth stops. Moving your head changes the viewpoint, so the tree feels three-dimensional.
