> Site-specific Soundscape Installation 2021, Venezia, Italy

This project is a site-specific composition of contemporary music and acousmatic design for the 60th anniversary exhibition of Pinot Grigio Santa Margherita.

The event took place at the 16th-century church S. Francesco Della Vigna in Venice. The two cloisters were used as commemorative installation space and press aperitif space. From that architecture we designed a two-layer soundscape to guide guests through the cloisters — letting them locate the source of the sounds and keeping a continuous sense of space.

{% include vimeo.html src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/591565838?h=37671d1c49&title=0&byline=0&portrait=0" title="Taste of Pinot" %}

![Presentation](/assets/works/taste-of-pinot/slide.jpg)

In the first cloister, the history of the Pinot is retraced through the winds of the vineyards and the pouring of wine. In the second, a smooth jazz session, layered on the previous cloister’s sounds, enriches the aperitif area — condensing that history into the glass about to be tasted.

![Cloister](/assets/works/taste-of-pinot/cloister.jpg)

Three speaker locations carry two layers: the first layer at speakers 1 and 2, the second at speaker 3, so the exhibition cloister and the aperitivo cloister share a related but different mix.
