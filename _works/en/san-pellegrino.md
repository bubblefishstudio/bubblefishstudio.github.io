This project is an unofficial composition & sound design synchronization for the advertisement of the sparkling water brand "San Pellegrino". We've been provided with the mute video and we created from scratch the whole audio experience.

This is the final project for the course "Music Production Technologies" from MSc degree in Music and Acoustic Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.
