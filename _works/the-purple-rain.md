> Generative Soundscape, Multimedia Installation 2022, Online

*The Purple Rain* is an audio-visual installation that reflects between inner human emotions and an ordinary scenery on a spring afternoon.

The work was made with sound synthesis in Max/MSP, sound design, and video editing.
